Unit 1 Question Bank

Unit 1 — Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

  1. Define Artificial Intelligence (AI) and differentiate between natural intelligence and artificial intelligence.
  2. Describe the logical approach to Artificial Intelligence. Explain why logic is important for reasoning in AI systems.
  3. Define a proposition in propositional logic and list any four logical connectives along with their respective symbols.
  4. State any two differences between propositional logic and first-order logic (predicate logic).
  5. Express the following statements in first-order logic: (a) Every human is mortal. (b) At least one student is intelligent.
  6. Explain the meaning of a proposition. Give two examples of propositions and two examples of non-propositions, providing a reason for each classification.
  7. Define a predicate, a universal quantifier, and an existential quantifier. Write the symbol for each and provide one verbal example of each.
  8. Using the predicates Teacher(x), Student(y), and Likes(y, x), represent the statement “Each teacher is liked by at least one student” in first-order logic.
