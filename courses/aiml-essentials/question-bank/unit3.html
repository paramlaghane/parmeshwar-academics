Unit 3 Question Bank

Unit 3 — Evolutionary Intelligence

  1. Q. No.: 1   What is an evolutionary algorithm? List its four main steps in the correct order and briefly explain each step.
  2. Q. No.: 2   Explain why evolutionary algorithms are considered population-based and bio-inspired search techniques. Give one reason why they can be used when the objective function is not smooth.
  3. Q. No.: 3  : Put the following genetic algorithm operations in the correct sequence and briefly explain each: population initialization, fitness evaluation, selection, crossover, mutation, and termination check.
  4. Q. No.: 4   Define elitism in genetic algorithms and mention one benefit of preserving the best individuals in successive generations.
  5. Q. No.: 5   Describe two-point crossover with a simple example. Also, state how it differs from single-point crossover. Type: Short Answer / Example BTL: BTL-2 CO: CO3 Marks: 4
  6. Q. No.: 6   Consider Parent 1 = 101010 and Parent 2 = 010101. Perform two-point crossover at positions 2 and 4 and determine the two resulting offspring.
  7. Q. No.: 7   What is the mutation operator in a genetic algorithm? Explain why the mutation probability is generally maintained at a low value.
  8. Q. No.: 8   Perform bit-flip mutation on the binary string 11001 by changing the bit at the fourth position. Write the resulting binary string.
  9. Q. No.: 9   Explain the concept of an optimization problem. Define an objective function and constraints, illustrating both with a simple example.
  10. Q. No.: 10  In the travelling salesman problem, identify the quantity to be minimized and describe the form of a candidate solution represented in a genetic algorithm.
