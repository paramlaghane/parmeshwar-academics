Internal Assessment 3

Topic Presentation

Course : AIML Essentials

Assessment Type : Internal Assessment

Activity : Topic Presentation

Instructions

  1. Choose ONE topic from the syllabus.
  2. Prepare a presentation (maximum 8 slides) on your chosen topic.
  3. Present it and Submit the hard copy of your presentation slides (print of the actual slides used) on the day of your presentation.( Deadline : 29 August. 26)

Note

Keep your presentation clear, concise, and focused on the selected topic. The content should be your own work, and all external sources used must be properly cited.
