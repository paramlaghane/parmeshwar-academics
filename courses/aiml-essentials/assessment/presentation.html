Assessment Component 3 – Topic Presentation

Assessment Component 3

Topic Presentation

Course : AIML Essentials

Assessment Type : Internal Assessment

Activity : Topic Presentation

Maximum Slides : 8

Deadline : 29 August 2026

Question

Choose ONE topic from the AIML Essentials syllabus and prepare a presentation on the selected topic.

The presentation should explain the selected topic clearly, concisely, and in a well-organized manner.

Instructions

  1. Choose ONE topic from the AIML Essentials syllabus.
  2. Prepare a presentation containing a maximum of 8 slides.
  3. Present the prepared presentation on the scheduled presentation day.
  4. Submit the hard copy of your presentation slides on the day of your presentation.
  5. The hard copy submitted must be a printout of the actual slides used for the presentation.
  6. Keep the presentation clear, concise, and focused on the selected topic.
  7. The presentation content should be your own work.
  8. All external sources used for preparing the presentation must be properly cited.

Presentation Guidelines

  • Select a topic that is clearly related to the AIML Essentials syllabus.
  • Use short points instead of long paragraphs wherever possible.
  • Use diagrams, flowcharts, examples, or illustrations wherever they help explain the concept.
  • Arrange the content logically so that the topic is easy to understand.
  • Use readable fonts and an appropriate slide layout.
  • Avoid unnecessary text and excessive animations.
  • Make sure that all technical terms are used correctly.
  • Include references or source citations for information, images, diagrams, or other external material used in the presentation.

Suggested Slide Structure

The following structure may be used as a guideline. Students may modify the structure according to the selected topic.

Slide 1 – Title

Topic name, student's name, roll number, course name, and other required details.

Slide 2 – Introduction

Brief introduction and importance of the selected topic.

Slide 3 – Key Concepts

Explain the important concepts, components, or terminology related to the topic.

Slide 4 – Working / Process

Explain how the concept, algorithm, or technique works using suitable steps, diagrams, or flowcharts.

Slide 5 – Example

Provide a suitable example to demonstrate the concept.

Slide 6 – Applications

Explain important real-world applications or use cases.

Slide 7 – Advantages / Limitations

Discuss important advantages, limitations, challenges, or considerations, wherever applicable.

Slide 8 – Conclusion and References

Summarize the key points and provide the references/sources used for preparing the presentation.

Important Note

The maximum number of slides is 8. Students should ensure that the presentation remains concise and focused on the selected topic.

Students must present the slides and submit a hard copy of the actual presentation slides used on the day of the presentation.

The presentation deadline is 29 August 2026.

All external sources used in the presentation must be properly cited.

Submission Checklist

  • ☐ Selected one topic from the AIML Essentials syllabus.
  • ☐ Presentation contains no more than 8 slides.
  • ☐ Presentation content is clear and concise.
  • ☐ Presentation has been prepared using original work.
  • ☐ External sources have been properly cited.
  • ☐ Presentation slides are ready for presentation.
  • ☐ Hard copy of the actual slides used has been prepared.
  • ☐ Presentation and hard-copy submission will be completed by 29 August 2026.
