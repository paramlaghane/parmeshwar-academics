AI & ML Essentials

Semester V • Theory Course

Course Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming modern computing by enabling machines to perform tasks that traditionally require human intelligence. This course introduces the theoretical foundations of Artificial Intelligence, probabilistic reasoning, evolutionary computation, machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and generative AI.


Students will explore intelligent problem-solving techniques, understand how machines learn from data, and study modern AI applications across healthcare, finance, education, manufacturing, cybersecurity, and robotics.

Course Outcomes

  • Explain the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence.
  • Apply logical and probabilistic reasoning techniques.
  • Understand evolutionary computation and optimization.
  • Build a strong foundation in Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
  • Explain Natural Language Processing techniques and Generative AI models.
  • Identify real-world applications and ethical implications of Artificial Intelligence.

Course Contents

Unit 1 – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Study Time: 9 Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the evolution of Artificial Intelligence, early AI concepts, and major milestones in AI development.
  • Learn logical reasoning using Propositional Logic, First-Order Logic, and Knowledge Representation techniques.
  • Study Rule-Based Systems, Knowledge-Based Systems, Inference Engines, and Case-Based Reasoning.
  • Explore practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare, finance, robotics, education, and smart systems.
Study Unit →

Unit 2 – Probabilistic Approaches to Artificial Intelligence

Study Time: 9 Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Understand probability concepts used in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent decision making.
  • Learn Bayes' Theorem, Bayesian Inference, Bayesian Networks, and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC).
  • Study Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs), Conditional Probability Tables (CPTs), and inference techniques.
  • Explore parameter learning, structure learning, and practical applications of Bayesian reasoning.
Study Unit →

Unit 3 – Evolutionary Intelligence

Study Time: 9 Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Understand Evolutionary Algorithms and their role in solving optimization problems.
  • Learn Genetic Algorithms including selection, crossover, mutation, and fitness evaluation.
  • Study Evolutionary Strategies, self-adaptation techniques, and different evolutionary models.
  • Explore Genetic Programming, tree-based program representation, and real-world optimization applications.
Study Unit →

Unit 4 – Machine Learning and Deep Learning

Study Time: 9 Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Understand supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, and reinforcement learning techniques used in Machine Learning.
  • Learn regression, decision trees, random forests, clustering, principal component analysis (PCA), and model evaluation methods.
  • Study Neural Networks, activation functions, feedforward networks, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs).
  • Understand transfer learning, deep learning applications, overfitting, underfitting, and model selection strategies.
Study Unit →

Unit 5 – Natural Language Processing and Generative AI

Study Time: 9 Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Learn Natural Language Processing (NLP), text preprocessing, tokenization, stopword removal, and word embeddings.
  • Understand sentiment analysis, text classification, named entity recognition, transformer-based language models, and text generation.
  • Study Generative Artificial Intelligence, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs).
  • Explore real-world applications and ethical considerations associated with Generative AI technologies.
