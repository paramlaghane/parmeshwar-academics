Unit 2 Question Bank

Unit 2 — Probabilistic Approaches to AI

  1. Q. No.: 1  State the formula for conditional probability and explain the meaning of P(A | B). If P(A ∩ B) = 0.12 and P(B) = 0.40, calculate P(A | B).
  2. Q. No.:2   Box A contains 3 red balls and 2 blue balls, while Box B contains 1 red ball and 4 blue balls. One box is selected randomly, and a red ball is drawn. Apply Bayes’ theorem to determine the probability that the selected ball came from Box A.
  3. Q. No.: 3   Explain the meaning of Bayesian inference. Identify the three inputs required for Bayesian inference and mention the result it provides.
  4. Q. No.: 4   Differentiate between a discrete random variable and a continuous random variable, giving one example of each.
  5. Q. No.: 5   Define a Bayesian network and describe its two essential components: its structure and its parameters.
  6. Q. No.: 6   What information is provided by a Conditional Probability Table (CPT) for a node in a Bayesian network? Explain why its probabilities depend on the node’s parents rather than directly on all other nodes.
  7. Q. No.: 7  State Bayes’ theorem and consider a rain-and-traffic scenario where P(Rain) = 0.30, P(Traffic | Rain) = 0.80, and P(Traffic | No Rain) = 0.25. Identify the prior, likelihood, and evidence terms, and state which term must be calculated first.
  8. Q. No.: 8  In a city, the probability of rain is 0.30, the probability of traffic given rain is 0.80, and the probability of traffic given no rain is 0.25. If a traffic jam occurs, use Bayes’ theorem to calculate P(Rain | Traffic).
  9. Q. No.: 9   A student claims: “Because P(Traffic | Rain) = 0.80, the probability of rain given a traffic jam must also be 0.80.” Given P(Rain) = 0.30, P(Traffic | Rain) = 0.80, and P(Traffic | No Rain) = 0.25, determine whether the claim is correct. Justify your answer in two or three lines, referring to 0.80 and 0.30.
  10. Q. No.: 10  In a city, P(Rain) = 0.30, P(Traffic | Rain) = 0.80, and P(Traffic | No Rain) = 0.25. If no traffic jam is observed, use Bayes’ theorem to calculate P(Rain | No Traffic). State briefly how the result differs from the prior probability of 0.30.
