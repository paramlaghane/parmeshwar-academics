Semester V • Laboratory Course

Course Overview

This laboratory course complements the theory course by providing hands-on experience in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Students will implement logic, probability, Bayesian reasoning, decision trees, and neural networks through practical experiments using Python and suitable software tools.

Pre-requisites

  • Basic knowledge of Python Programming.
  • Basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence concepts.
  • Ability to write simple Python programs.
  • Familiarity with logical reasoning and mathematical concepts.

Course Objectives

  • Develop conceptual understanding of AI and Machine Learning fundamentals.
  • Build analytical skills for reasoning under uncertainty.
  • Understand optimization using evolutionary techniques.
  • Evaluate Machine Learning models.
  • Gain practical knowledge of Neural Networks, NLP and Generative AI.
  • Understand ethical and responsible AI practices.

Course Outcomes

  • CO1: Explain the fundamental principles and evolution of Artificial Intelligence.
  • CO2: Apply logical and probabilistic reasoning to Artificial Intelligence problems.
  • CO3: Analyze evolutionary algorithms for optimization tasks.
  • CO4: Distinguish different Machine Learning models and learning paradigms.
  • CO5: Describe Generative AI models and their applications.
